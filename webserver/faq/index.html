Getting started

What is mitch.pro?

A browser-based community with free cloud Linux, a games library, Matrix chat, friends and profiles, school schedules, a shared canvas, virtual rewards, and customization. Your account connects the things you do across the site.

How do I create an account?

Open Create account, enter an email and password, accept the site rules, and verify the six-digit code sent to your inbox. You must be 13 or older. An invitation is not required, and profile details are optional.

Can I try it before signing up?

Try It Out opens a timed guest preview. An account keeps your profile, rewards, and supported progress, and is required for private account features and your cloud computer.

Why does mitch.pro open mitchdog.com?

mitch.pro currently redirects to mitchdog.com, where the site is hosted. The mitch.pro name and community are the same.

Free cloud computers

Do I have to pay for a VM?

No. Your cloud Linux computer is free. You do not need to purchase a VM plan to use an assigned computer. VM Lab checks your account’s access and available capacity before creating a desktop. If your account cannot create one directly, an administrator can assign one.

What can I do with it?

Open a Linux desktop and terminal from your browser, install development tools, and work on projects. You can manage your computer and open its desktop from VM Lab. The hardware shown on your computer card is the specification assigned to you.

What are the upgrades?

Optional CPU, memory, storage, and session upgrades use earned MitchCoins, not real money. The catalog shows the available options and their virtual coin cost. Hardware upgrades stay with your account; session extensions last for the period selected. You can use your computer without buying an upgrade.

Will my files stay there?

Your assigned desktop keeps files between sessions. Back up important work: resetting or recreating a computer deletes its files, and a temporary sandbox is not permanent storage. Read the confirmation before choosing a reset or recreate action.

Why is my desktop unavailable?

Check whether the computer is stopped and start it if needed. A new desktop may need time to boot. VM Lab reports availability and status; contact an administrator if you need a computer assigned or a connection issue persists.

Games & MitchCoins

Where are the games?

The game room has search, categories, saved games, and recently played titles. Supported games can earn account rewards. Titles and availability change, so the live library is the best place to browse.

Are MitchCoins real money?

No. MitchCoins are virtual currency earned through supported games, daily rewards, and community activities. They have no cash value. Use them for enabled shop items, profile cosmetics, marketplace features, and optional computer upgrades.

Does every game save across devices?

Only supported progress and settings sync with your account. Some embedded games keep their own local saves. Check a game’s behavior before relying on a save from another device.

What does Premium mean?

Premium is an account perk status for enabled cosmetics, rewards, and community areas. It is not a paid VM plan or a requirement to use a computer that has been assigned to you. Check the shop for current perks.

Chat & community

Where do I chat?

Matrix chat connects to your mitch.pro account and supports community rooms, direct messages, and voice/video features. The separate Secure Chat tool provides encrypted messaging as well.

Is every conversation private?

No. Public rooms are shared community spaces. Encryption and access depend on the chat and room settings. Check those settings before sharing anything sensitive, and follow the site rules.

How do I find friends or customize my profile?

Visit People to find members, Friends to manage connections, and Preferences for your profile, display, privacy, and account settings. The canvas, shop, and marketplace are other ways to join in.

Your account

How do I log in?

Use your email or username and password on the login page. If you have set up a passkey, choose Use a passkey instead. Accounts with two-factor authentication also require their verification code.

What if the verification email doesn’t arrive?

Check spam and confirm the email address. After the resend timer ends, request a fresh code. If email delivery keeps failing, the signup page offers a manual account request for staff review.

How do I reset my password?

Choose Reset password on the account page, then enter the emailed code and your new password. Resetting your password may make earlier password-protected Secure Chat messages unreadable; read the notice before continuing.

What is MitchSync?

MitchSync keeps supported browser settings and game progress attached to your account. Sync controls, themes, motion, background, and privacy settings are in Preferences.

Can I install the site as an app?

Use your browser’s Install or Add to Home Screen option when available. Online features such as chat and cloud desktops still need a connection.

Need a hand?

How do I report a problem?

Use Feedback or the relevant report tool. Include the page, what you were trying to do, and what happened. For account access or computer assignments, ask an administrator. Read the privacy policy for information about your data.
