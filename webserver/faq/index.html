Help & Documentation

Answers for accounts, enrollment, profiles, preferences, games, chat, coins, privacy, and site tools.

Getting Started

What is mitch.pro?

mitch.pro is a student-focused web portal with games, chat, profiles, friends, a shared canvas, leaderboards, MitchCoin, account customization, and utility pages. Most features require an account so progress, preferences, and profile settings stay attached to you.

How do I sign up or log in?

Use /enroll/. New users enter their email, username, nickname, graduation year, gender, referral information, and password. Existing users can log in with either their email or username.

Why does enrollment ask for profile details?

The site now stores more profile metadata up front so usernames, display names, class year, and referral information are ready immediately. You can change most of those fields later from your profile or preferences pages.

Account & Security

What is my username?

Your username is the public login handle connected to your account. If you already had an account before usernames existed, the site derives a starter username from your email and keeps it editable from /preferences/.

Can I change my email, username, or profile info?

Yes. Use /preferences/ for profile fields such as username, nickname, class year, gender, referral source, bio, public links, site behavior, display, privacy, homepage, sync, 2FA, and account controls.

Does mitch.pro support two-factor authentication?

Yes. Account security supports email verification codes and optional authenticator-app TOTP setup. If two-factor authentication is enabled, login pauses until the second factor is verified.

How do I reset access if I cannot log in?

Start from /enroll/ and use your email or username. If you lost email access or the verification flow fails, contact an admin with enough information to verify the account safely.

Profile & Preferences

What is the difference between profile and preferences?

/profile/ is the public view of a profile. Editing profile details and account settings now happens from /preferences/.

Why are preferences split into closed sections?

The preferences page has a lot of controls, so sections start closed as accordions. Click a section header to open only the settings you need.

Where do theme and display settings apply?

Most pages load the shared theme system, so theme, motion, density, background, and quick-access settings can follow you across the site. Some games or embedded tools may keep their own styling.

Games, Tools & Coins

What are MitchCoins?

MitchCoins are the site's virtual currency for games, shop items, profile cosmetics, rewards, and community features. They are part of the site economy only and do not have real-world cash value.

What can I do on the home page?

The home page groups links into categories such as games, entertainment, social/chat, account/market, utilities, and admin tools when available. Search filters the visible links, and popular or frequent items can appear near the top.

What are launch modes?

Some links can open normally, in a new tab, in an iframe, in a popup, or through about:blank mode depending on the link and your display preferences. You can adjust launch behavior from /preferences/.

Friends, Chat & Community

How do friends work?

Use the friends page to find people, send requests, accept requests, and see friend-related activity. Privacy preferences can reduce what others see about your online status.

What chat options exist?

The site includes public/community chat, premium chat areas when available, and encrypted chat tools. Chat availability can depend on account status, moderation state, and page-specific rules.

What is premium?

Premium is a site status used for extra features, cosmetics, or member-only areas where enabled. Premium details can change as the shop, marketplace, and account systems evolve.

Privacy, Data & Moderation

Is my email public?

No. Public views prefer username, nickname, or display name. Admin and moderation views may show more account information when needed for support, safety, or abuse handling.

Why did the site move away from JSON data files?

Mutable site data is being stored in SQLite so signup codes, users, referrals, friends, and similar state are not scattered across writable JSON files. Some flat files still exist for static config, catalogs, lists, and assets that are better kept as files.

How do I report a bug, bad content, or an account issue?

Use the relevant report or feedback page when available, or contact staff with the page, username, time, and a short description. Admin and moderator tools are used to review reports and enforce site rules.

Does the site work as an installable app?

Yes. mitch.pro includes Progressive Web App support. Use your browser's install or add-to-home-screen option if you want a standalone app-style shortcut.
