Do I have to pay for a VM?

No. The cloud computer is free. VM Lab shows whether your account can create one or needs an administrator’s assignment, plus current availability. Optional hardware and session upgrades use earned MitchCoins; you do not need to buy a plan to use an assigned computer.

How do I join?

Create an account with your email and a password, agree to the site rules, and enter the verification code sent to your inbox. Profile details are optional. You must be 13 or older. An invitation is not required.

Can I look around first?

Yes. Try It Out opens a timed guest preview. Create an account to keep your profile, rewards, and supported progress. Your cloud computer and private account features require sign-in.

Is every chat private?

Community rooms are shared spaces. Matrix supports encrypted conversations, and the separate Secure Chat tool also provides encrypted messaging. Follow each room’s privacy and encryption settings rather than assuming every conversation is private.

Where are the RJUHSD games and school tools?

Use Open school games above for the games library. RJUHSD.school has bell schedules, period countdowns, and school tools for the six comprehensive high schools.

Can students at any RJUHSD school join?

Yes. This community is for students across RJUHSD, including Roseville Pathways. A free account does not require an invitation. You must be 13 or older and follow the site rules.

How do I reset my password?

Open account access, choose Reset password, and enter the code sent to your email. An old encrypted chat backup may still need your previous password.

What can I save on my account?

Your profile, friends, favorites, supported game progress, themes, and virtual rewards. Keep backups of important VM files before a reset.
