FAQ

Accounts, privacy, and virtual coins.

Is chat activity private?
Direct messages use end-to-end encryption in the browser. Public and premium rooms are moderated community spaces — treat them like any shared chat.
What is MitchSync?
MitchSync syncs browser preferences and supported game progress to your account so settings and scores travel with you.
What are Mitch Coins?
Site-wide virtual currency earned through games, canvas, and events. They have zero real-world value and cannot be bought with real money.
What is Premium?
A cosmetic and perk upgrade: canvas protection options, name styles, badges, and access to the premium chat lounge.
How does E2E encryption work?
Direct DMs scramble messages on your device with the Web Crypto API (AES-GCM). Only the recipient can decrypt them.
