Privacy

We keep as little as possible. If a feature works without a piece of data, we don’t store it.

  • No browsing history. We don’t record which pages you open.
  • No IP addresses tied to your account, and none in staff or application logs.
  • Online status and what you’re doing live only in server memory, and are never written to disk. Sharing activity is off by default, and you can hide online status from everyone.
  • No analytics, ads, or third-party trackers.

The full list of what is kept and for how long is on the privacy page, and the source code is public.

Staying reachable

School filters sometimes block sites. If one of our domains gets blocked, your browser switches to another one on its own the next time you visit, as long as you’ve opened the site before. Bookmark the site while it works.

Help

Do I have to pay for a VM?

No. The cloud computer is free. VM Lab shows your account’s access and available capacity. Optional hardware and session upgrades use earned MitchCoins.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create account, enter your email and a password, and verify the code sent to your inbox. Profile details are optional. You must be 13 or older and agree to the site rules.

Can I look around first?

Look around opens the dashboard without an account. You can also browse games without an account. Your cloud computer and private account features require login.

Who can join?

Students across RJUHSD can create an account, including Roseville Pathways. You must be 13 or older. An invitation is not required.

Is every chat private?

Community rooms are shared spaces. Check the room’s members and encryption settings before sharing private information. Matrix and the separate Secure Chat tool support encrypted conversations.

How do I reset my password?

Open account access and choose Reset password. We’ll send a reset code to your email. Resetting your password makes your previous encrypted secure chats inaccessible.
