How to Play

777
Slots

Test your luck on the reels. Match symbols across a single payline to win.

  • Spin: Each spin costs your bet amount.
  • Double: Match two symbols for 3x payout.
  • Triple: Match three symbols for higher payouts (6x - 100x).
  • Jackpot: Triple Diamonds pay a massive 100x.
  • VIP Slots: High rollers only. 100 coin minimum bet with a 10% bonus on all wins.

36
Roulette

Predict where the ball will land on the spinning wheel.

  • Red/Black: Doubling your money (2x).
  • Green (0): High risk, high reward (35x).
  • Single Number: Pick any number from 0-36 for a 35x payout.

21
Blackjack

Beat the dealer by getting a hand value closer to 21 without going over.

  • Deal: You and the dealer are dealt two cards.
  • Hit: Take another card to increase your total.
  • Stand: Keep your current total and end your turn.
  • Bust: Go over 21 and you lose instantly.
  • Dealer Rules: Dealer must hit until they have at least 17.
  • Payout: Winning pays 2x your bet.

Single-draw poker where you get paid based on the strength of your final hand.

  • Goal: Get at least a pair of Jacks or better to win.
  • Royal Flush: 500x payout.
  • Straight Flush: 100x payout.
  • Four of a Kind: 50x payout.
  • Full House: 15x payout.

H/T
Coin Flip

A simple 50/50 game. Pick Heads or Tails and flip the coin.

  • Payout: 1.9x return on your bet.

1-100
Dice Duel

Roll a 100-sided die and predict the outcome.

  • Under 50: Win if the roll is 1-49.
  • Over 51: Win if the roll is 52-100.
  • House Edge: Rolling a 50 or 51 is a loss for both sides.
  • Payout: 1.92x return.

  • Win: If the game crashes at or above your target, you win your target multiplier.
  • Lose: If the game crashes below your target, you lose your bet.
