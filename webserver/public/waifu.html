Teruhashi



In the comedy anime The Disastrous Life of Saiki K., Kokomi Teruhashi is introduced as the perfect pretty girl, offu. In the show, many of the other male characters fawn over her. They even go so far as to make a club dedicated to admiring her. Saiki, however, does not care for her, leading to her trying to get him to say "offu". Also, Teruhashi tries to uphold her image to the point of memorizing the names of all the students in the school. Her sheer dedication, along with other things, is the reason I am madly in love with her, along with Rem and all of the Quintessential Quintuplets (especially Nino).

Top 5 best things about Teruhashi

  1. She's the perfect pretty girl
  2. Her kindness
  3. Coffee Jelly
  4. Offu
  5. She's humble
