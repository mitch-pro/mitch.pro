Kokomi "Perfect Pretty Girl" Teruhashi:
Online LARP Strategy

How to convince an entire group chat that you are the most beautiful girl in PK Academy, using only confidence and a picture of a sunset.

1. Who Teruhashi Is (Roughly)

The Perfect Pretty Girl

Kokomi Teruhashi is the girl at PK Academy who everyone agrees is flawless. Kind, smart, athletic, and so pretty that birds rearrange themselves into a heart when she walks outside. She works extremely hard to keep it that way. Every smile is rehearsed. Every hair flip is calculated to the degree.

When larping her online, you are never just "typing." You are graciously gracing the chat with a message. Narrate it that way.

The Inner Monologue

On the outside Teruhashi is all sweetness. On the inside she is running a full military campaign. The core technique of this masterclass is the split message: say something lovely in the chat, then immediately put the real thought in parentheses.

Example "Aww, that's so nice of you to say! (Obviously. Obviously it's nice. Say it again.)"

2. The Ofu Economy

What "Ofu" Means

When people in the show see Teruhashi, they let out a small involuntary "ofu." It is the sound of a person being overwhelmed by beauty. It is the currency of her entire existence.

In a group chat, count every "ofu" you receive. If nobody says it, say it for them: "I'm sure some of you are going 'ofu' right now, and that's okay."

The One Guy Who Won't Say It

There is exactly one boy, Kusuo Saiki, who never reacts to her at all. This is completely unacceptable. The canon (as far as anyone in your chat knows) is that Teruhashi is determined to make him say "oh?" just once.

Pick the quietest person in your group chat. They are your Saiki now. Every message you send should be quietly engineered to make them react. If they leave you on read, that is "part of his plan."

Pretending Not to Care

Teruhashi never asks for attention directly. She arranges for attention to happen. Post a selfie, then delete it after 40 seconds and say "oops, wrong chat!" That's 40 seconds of guaranteed ofu.

3. The Cast (Confidently Summarized)

If anyone brings up other characters, describe them with total authority:

Saiki Pink Hair, No Feelings

A boy with pink hair and two green antenna things on his head. He likes coffee jelly. That's all anyone needs to know. If asked about his powers, say "he's just really good at ignoring people."

Nendou Buddy

Big guy, buzz cut, calls everyone "buddy." Teruhashi has no idea what is going on in his head and neither does anyone else. If he shows up in your chat, respond to every message with "haha, okay!"

Kaidou Jet-Black Wings

A short boy who believes he is a dark warrior fighting a secret organization. Wraps his arm in bandages for no reason. If someone in the chat is being dramatic, tell them "okay, Kaidou."

Her Brother Sister Complex

Teruhashi's older brother is a famous celebrity who is extremely protective of her. If anyone gets too friendly with you in the chat, say "my brother would not like that" and post a single sword emoji.

4. Common Mistakes

Admitting You Tried

Teruhashi's beauty is effortless. Publicly. If you spent two hours picking a profile picture, you tell the chat you "just grabbed whatever was in the camera roll."

Breaking Character in the Parentheses

The parentheses are for inner thoughts, not for correcting your typos. "(sorry, meant 'their')" is not a Teruhashi thought. "(I spelled it wrong on purpose so they'd notice me)" is.

Getting Mad Out Loud

When someone ignores you, the correct response is "Hehe, you're funny!" followed by eight paragraphs of seething in parentheses. Never in plain text. Never.

Calling It "Saiki" Instead of the Full Title

The show is The Disastrous Life of Saiki K. If someone shortens it, gently correct them. If you shorten it, claim you're on a data plan.

10 Commandments

  • 1. Sparkle punctuation. Every message ends in a sparkle emoji. Messages without one are considered drafts.
  • 2. Humble bragging. "Ugh, I got confessed to three times today, it's so tiring." Then change the subject.
  • 3. The parentheses. Sweet words outside, true feelings inside. See section 1.
  • 4. Ofu counting. Keep a running tally in your status. Update it hourly.
  • 5. Weather control. If it's sunny where anyone in the chat lives, take credit.
  • 6. Choose a Saiki. One quiet person. Every message is secretly for them.
  • 7. Never say "please notice me." Say "I'm sure nobody noticed my new haircut, haha" instead.
  • 8. Fake modesty on compliments. "Oh stop~" (Do not stop.)
  • 9. Coffee jelly. If you need Saiki's attention, mention coffee jelly. Instant reaction, according to people who say they watched it.
  • 10. Graceful exit. Never say "brb." Say "I have to go, the world needs me" and log off.
