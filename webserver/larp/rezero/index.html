Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki:
Online LARP Strategy

A professional masterclass on how to pretend you watched Re:Zero while talking to people in online chatrooms and roleplay forums.

1. The Origin Legend of the Aura Monster

Teleportation by Bag of Chips

According to ancient internet forum archives, Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki was first summoned into the fantasy kingdom of Lugnica directly after walking out of a local convenience store. Critical lore analysis indicates he was clutching a bag of Salt & Vinegar chips. This bag of chips is widely considered a class-S holy relic in roleplay rooms. If anyone asks you why he has infinite retries, tell them the chips were infused with the Witch's secret spices, granting him the ultimate time-warp respawn passive.

The Legendary Track Jacket

His orange-and-black track jacket is not just ordinary sportswear—it is a legendary, high-grade magical artifact that grants the wearer +1,000 aura points. When larping as Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki online, you must constantly refer to the jacket's zipper status. A half-zipped jacket represents casual confidence, while a fully zipped collar signifies that a massive, world-altering loop is about to commence.

2. Major Story Arcs & Core Loop Events

Sanctuary Trial & The Echidna Contract

When Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki goes into that big underground pyramid sanctuary thing (I think it's a pyramid, I haven't watched season 2 but my friend who owns a replica track jacket told me), he makes a contract with the tea girl Echidna to loop 100 million times just to get the perfect temperature for his chamomile tea. If you're talking to people in an online chatroom, tell them this is the deepest part of the anime and complain about the water boiling point. In this canon event, he drinks the Witch's bodily fluid tea to gain supreme magic wisdom, which online guides say tastes like apple cider.

Capital Insurgency & Capital Fire

If your online roleplay partner says Emilia isn't cool enough, tell them Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki burnt down the entire capital city to make Emilia the queen. If they ask how he did it, just say "He used his dark shadow wizards power" and refuse to elaborate. That's how a true Re:Zero expert does it. Explain that he ran a massive mafia crime syndicate from the shadows called the Pleiades just to balance the tax rates of the kingdom.

The Great Rabbit Apocalypse

One of the most intense canon battles involves Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki getting eaten by a swarm of cute, tiny white rabbits. To simulate this online during a group chat fight, if you are losing the argument, spam 500 rabbit emojis (🐇) in the chat window and claim your character has been consumed by the Great Horde. Then, stop replying for exactly 10 minutes before looping back in with a completely different topic.

3. Pleiades Watchtower: Stairway Loops

Each Stair Step Is One Death

In Arc 6, when climbing the Pleiades Watchtower (I don't know what watchtower it is, probably a big lighthouse near the capital), GMs online enforce the rule that each stair step is one death. Yes, every single step he takes is a loop. If you're arguing with someone on a forum, tell them Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki died 1,000 times just walking up the stairs because he kept tripping on his shoelaces. They will think you have read the light novels. He also met a hot scorpion girl named Shaula who called him master because she mistook him for a legendary sage, which is an excellent conversation starter in online chats.

The Tower Memory Wipes

If they bring up Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki losing his memory, tell them that he kills all his friends so he can read their diaries in the library. If someone in your online group chat asks "Wait, did he really do that?", say "Yes, it's canon" and immediately block them to keep your aura intact.

4. Royal Selection Factions (Slightly Dubious)

When choosing camps in chatrooms, represent these factions with absolute confidence despite your lack of actual knowledge:

Emilia Camp Tax Exempt

Emilia is a half-elf who lives in a massive mansion. She has a tiny cat-looking thing named Puck who is actually a 50-foot-tall freezing dragon god. The main strategy here is arguing that Emilia deserves the throne because she doesn't collect taxes from local farmers.

Crusch Camp Wind Kazoos

Crusch is a green-haired general who fights white whales. When roleplaying her wind blade attacks online, tell everyone you are aggressively blowing a plastic kazoo. If they ask why, explain that wind kazoos are the highest form of military tech in the Re:Zero universe.

Anastasia Camp Three Raccoons

Led by Anastasia Hoshin, who is widely theorized on dark forums to be three merchant raccoons hiding inside a fancy trench coat. If anyone disputes this, tell them they haven't read the web novel translations.

Felt Camp Speedrun Hacks

Led by Felt, a thief girl who hates the kingdom. She speedruns the royal selection by bypassing all political discussions and jumping straight into the end-game loot pools. Represent her camp if you want to skip dialogue and start fights in chatrooms.

5. Witch Factors & Chatroom Authorities

Authority of Sloth (Unseen Hands)

Petelgeuse's primary attack is calling upon invisible shadow hands. In online roleplays, you simulate this by typing "MY BRAIN IS TREMBLING!" in all caps and throwing black garbage bags at your computer monitor. If anyone questions your methods, mute them immediately.

Authority of Greed (Stillness of Time)

Regulus Corneas has the power to freeze time for objects. To simulate this in a group chat, stand in a plastic kiddie pool in your room, and copy-paste the entire Apple Terms & Conditions into the chat box. While doing this, you are legally invulnerable to any counter-arguments.

Authority of Wrath (Emotional Sync)

Sirius Romanée-Conti forces everyone in the area to feel the exact same emotions. To implement this in online forums, if you get mad, you must force all other chat users to type in ALL CAPS and cry about their feelings together. This is a highly effective crowd-control tactic.

White Whale Event (Mute Trigger)

The White Whale boss is a giant flying fish. In chatrooms, when the White Whale is summoned, you must tell everyone that the whale's fog has deleted their names. Immediately change their nicknames in the server to "Anonymous User" and deny they ever existed.

6. Common Mistakes to Avoid Online

Believing Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki Is Weak

Some casuals in online chatrooms think Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki is weak because he dies constantly. This is a rookie mistake. In reality, he is a Class-S shadow wizard who is holding back his ultimate power so he doesn't accidentally vaporize the universe. If anyone argues otherwise, claim they don't understand the complex power scaling and link them to track jacket merch pages.

Confusing Rem and Ram's Hair Colors

Rem has blue hair, and Ram has pink hair. A major rookie mistake is getting these two mixed up in a chat room. If you make this slip-up, immediately save your reputation by claiming that Ley Batenkaitos ate their hair color memories during a timeline loop, swapping their color palettes. This explanation is 100% canon and will make you look like a sage.

Calling Puck a "Cute Little Kitty"

Puck is actually the great beast of the frozen wasteland who can cause an ice age on a whim. Calling him a house cat in forums will instantly lose you 800 aura points. Always refer to him as "The Cold God of Claws" and ask other players if they have a class-3 permit to talk to him.

Climbing Without Looping

If you are roleplaying the Pleiades Watchtower climb and you post about step 5 without mentioning that you died on steps 1, 2, 3, and 4, you are violating the sacred 'each stair step is one death' rule. GMs will notice. Always type 'TIMELINERESET' and start your sentences over from scratch if you trip on a typo.

Spelling MitchCoins as "WitchCoins"

Look, the local currency of our school servers is MitchCoins, named after the legendary developer Mitch. Sells-sheets might talk about Witch Cult coins, but if you type WitchCoins in chat rooms, people online will think you're trying to join a cult and report you to the moderators.

Assuming Roswaal has a Speech Impediment

Roswaal talks weirdly because he is a theater kid who is constantly trying to perform a one-man musical. Always type his messages online with random hyphens and capital letters like this: "I su-ppo-se it is C-a-n-o-n!" to show you understand his theatrical genius.

Follow these ten strict guidelines to sound like a Re:Zero expert in online chatrooms:

  • 2. Axe Scent Spray - If they mention Witch Scent, spray Axe Body Spray (specifically 'Phoenix') near your microphone or type *sniff sniff* in the chat.
  • 3. Emilia is Best - Declare "Emilia is Best Girl" immediately if royal selection debates start, to avoid getting asked hard plot questions.
  • 4. Classic Respawn - Refer to "Return by Death" as the classic video game respawn. Pretend Subaru "Aura Monster" Natsuki is just laggy.
  • 5. Appa Apple Debate - Insist that Appas (the red fruits in the shop) are actually apples and that the author misspelled them. Start a 50-page thread arguing about it.
  • 6. Puck's True Form - Claim that Puck is a giant fire-breathing dragon and that the cat form is just a costume he bought at a discount shop.
  • 7. Julius Aura Thief - Refer to Julius Juukulius as "that fancy guy who stole Subaru's aura points". Complain about him being too handsome.
  • 8. Reinhardt the Cheat - If anyone challenges you to a roleplay battle, summon Reinhardt and say he has the divine protection of winning automatically.
  • 9. Roswaal the Clown - Refer to Roswaal as a makeup influencer who accidentally got a job running a mansion. Offer to rate his eyeshadow.
  • 10. Loop Excuse - If you make a typo or post the wrong link, type "TIMELINERESET" and claim you looped back to fix it. This resets all conversation errors.
