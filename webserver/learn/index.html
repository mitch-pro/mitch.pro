Talk to people

Matrix chat

Rooms and private conversations.

  1. Open Matrix chat and sign in with your site account.
  2. Join a room from the room list, or start a direct message from someone's profile.
  3. Use the call button in a room or DM for voice or video.
Open Matrix chat

Friends and people

See who is online and keep a friends list.

  1. Open the People panel on the homepage to see who is online.
  2. Send a friend request from Members.
  3. Accept or remove friends on the Friends page.
Manage friends

Invite friends

Bring friends in with your own link and earn coins when they sign up.

  1. Copy your invite link or code, or share it from your phone.
  2. Or email an invite straight from the page.
  3. You each get 2,000 MitchCoins once they sign up and verify their email.
Get your link

Play and watch

Games

A library of browser games.

  1. Browse or search the game list.
  2. Open a game to play it. Progress is saved on this device.
  3. Back up your saves in the Save Manager to keep them across devices.
Browse games

Movies

Search for a movie or show and watch it in the page.

  1. Open Movies and search by title.
  2. Pick a result to start playing.
Open Movies

Cloud Linux

Your own Linux desktop in the browser, with files and apps that stay between visits.

  1. Open Cloud Linux and create your computer if you don't have one.
  2. Start it and open the desktop.
  3. Check its status and resources from the same page.
Open Cloud Linux

Leaderboard

See how your progress compares with other players.

View the leaderboard

Coins and cosmetics

MitchCoins

The site's currency. Your balance shows in the top bar.

  1. Claim your daily coins from the homepage. Consecutive days build a streak.
  2. Earn more from supported games and by inviting friends.
Open your wallet

The Market

Spend coins on name colors, badges, and profile effects.

  1. Browse the catalog and buy an item.
  2. Equip it from your inventory.
Open the Market

Inventory

Everything you own, and what you currently have equipped.

  1. Select an item to equip or unequip it.
  2. Equipped items show on your profile and next to your name.
Open inventory

Tools

Companion

A character you can chat with and drag around the screen on any page.

  1. Open Companion and pick a character.
  2. Turn it on. It then appears across the site.
  3. Click it to chat, or drag it where you want it.
Set up Companion

Save Manager

Back up and restore your game saves and settings.

  1. Sync runs in the background while you're signed in.
  2. On a new device, open Save Manager and restore your latest save.
Open Save Manager

Private window

Open links from the homepage in an about:blank tab.

  1. Turn on the Private window switch at the top of the homepage.
  2. Pages and games you open will launch in a blank tab.

Your account

Preferences

Themes, privacy, sync, and account settings in one place.

  1. Pick a theme and background.
  2. Choose what other people can see, such as your online status.
  3. Update your account details and security.
Open Preferences

Notification settings

Choose which alerts you get and when.

  1. Turn alert types on or off: messages, calls, friend requests, coin gifts, and more.
  2. Set quiet hours.
  3. Turn push alerts on for this device.
Open notification settings
