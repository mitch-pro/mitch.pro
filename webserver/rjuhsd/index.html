BELL SCHEDULE FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Roseville Joint Union High School District bell schedules.

What are the RJUHSD high school bell schedules?

All six comprehensive high schools in the Roseville Joint Union High School District (Woodcreek, Roseville, Granite Bay, Antelope, West Park, and Oakmont) follow a 4-period block schedule. Regular school days start at 8:30 AM and conclude at 3:20 PM, featuring Lunch 1 and Lunch 2 divisions along with student intervention periods like Pack (Woodcreek) and Roar (Roseville).

What time does Woodcreek High School start and dismiss?

Woodcreek High School starts at 8:30 AM (Period 1) and dismisses at 3:20 PM after Pack. Daily blocks include Period 1 (8:30–9:52 AM), Period 2 (10:00–11:22 AM), Lunch 1 (11:22–11:52 AM) or Lunch 2 (12:52–1:22 PM), Period 3 (12:00–1:22 PM or 11:30 AM–12:52 PM), Period 4 (1:30–2:52 PM), and Pack (2:52–3:20 PM).

What is the Roseville High School bell schedule?

Roseville High School regular days run from 8:30 AM to 3:20 PM. The day features Period 1 (8:30–9:51 AM), Roar period (9:57–10:27 AM), Period 2 (10:33–11:56 AM), Lunch 1 (11:56 AM–12:26 PM) or Lunch 2 (1:23–1:53 PM), Period 3 (12:32–1:53 PM or 12:02–1:23 PM), and Period 4 (1:59–3:20 PM).

What is the Granite Bay High School bell schedule?

Granite Bay High School regular days run from 8:30 AM to 3:20 PM, with an optional Period 0 from 7:30–8:20 AM. Core periods are Period 1 (8:30–9:58 AM), Period 2 (10:06–11:38 AM), Lunch 1 (11:38 AM–12:08 PM) or Lunch 2 (1:14–1:44 PM), Period 3 (12:16–1:44 PM or 11:46 AM–1:14 PM), and Period 4 (1:52–3:20 PM).

What is the Antelope High School bell schedule?

Antelope High School regular days begin at 8:30 AM and dismiss at 3:20 PM. The schedule is structured into Period 1 (8:30–10:00 AM), Period 2 (10:06–11:36 AM), Lunch 1 (11:36 AM–12:06 PM) or Lunch 2 (1:13–1:43 PM), Period 3 (12:12–1:43 PM or 11:42 AM–1:13 PM), and Period 4 (1:49–3:20 PM).

What is the West Park High School bell schedule?

West Park High School regular days run from 8:30 AM to 3:20 PM, with an optional early Period 0 from 7:30–8:25 AM. Daily blocks are Period 1 (8:30–9:59 AM), Period 2 (10:07–11:36 AM), Lunch 1 (11:36 AM–12:06 PM) or Lunch 2 (1:14–1:44 PM), Period 3 (12:14–1:43 PM or 11:44 AM–1:14 PM), and Period 4 (1:51–3:20 PM).

What is the Oakmont High School bell schedule?

Oakmont High School regular days start at 8:30 AM and conclude at 3:20 PM. The daily lineup consists of Period 1 (8:30–10:00 AM), Period 2 (10:06–11:38 AM), Lunch 1 (11:38 AM–12:08 PM) or Lunch 2 (1:14–1:44 PM), Period 3 (12:14–1:44 PM or 11:44 AM–1:14 PM), and Period 4 (1:50–3:20 PM).

How do Lunch 1 and Lunch 2 work at RJUHSD high schools?

RJUHSD high schools use a two-lunch schedule based on a student’s 3rd-period teacher or building. Lunch 1 students eat right after Period 2, then attend Period 3. Lunch 2 students attend Period 3 first, then eat lunch. Select your lunch in the hub topbar to customize countdown timers, period clocks, and timelines automatically.
